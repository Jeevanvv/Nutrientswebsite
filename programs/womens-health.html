What this is

Hormone-aware nutrition across every stage of a woman's life

The Women's Health Program addresses PCOS, thyroid disorders, insulin resistance, fertility, pregnancy, postpartum recovery and perimenopause through nutrition designed around hormonal physiology rather than calorie arithmetic.

This is Nutrinest's deepest area of expertise and its largest client base. Women with PCOS and thyroid disorders are routinely told to 'eat less and move more' — advice that ignores the insulin resistance driving the condition and frequently makes it worse. Hormone-aware nutrition is not a niche; it's the correct standard of care for a very large population.

How it runs

Delivered online through the coaching program structure, with weekly consultations, cycle-linked adjustment, bloodwork interpretation and stage-specific pathways from PCOS through perimenopause.

What this program sets out to do
  • Improve insulin sensitivity and hormonal markers through targeted nutrition.
  • Manage PCOS and thyroid symptoms with food, not just medication.
  • Support fertility, pregnancy and postpartum recovery at each distinct stage.
  • Correct the deficiencies that disproportionately affect Indian women.
  • Replace restrictive dieting with nutrition that works with the endocrine system.

Life stage pathways

The same body, five different sets of rules

Nutrition that ignores which stage you are in is nutrition that will not work. Pick yours.

What we work on

Insulin sensitivity first. Meal composition, sequencing and timing to stabilise glucose, adequate energy rather than restriction, and inflammation load.

What we track

Fasting insulin, HbA1c, lipids, vitamin D, ferritin, cycle length and regularity, acne, hair, energy and body composition — not weight alone.

What we do not do

Prescribe a 1,200-calorie diet to an insulin-resistant body and call the resulting fatigue a lack of discipline.

What we work on

Nutrition that works with your medication rather than against it: timing around the dose, iodine and selenium adequacy, iron and B12 repletion, and absorption.

What we track

Full thyroid panel, ferritin, B12, vitamin D, energy, cold tolerance, digestion, hair and body composition across the treatment period.

What we do not do

Change your dose, tell you to stop your medication, or promise a diet that removes the need for it.

What we work on

Preconception repletion for both partners where relevant, ovulation support, then trimester-specific planning with gestational diabetes risk managed early.

What we track

Cycle markers, insulin and glucose, vitamin D, B12, iron, thyroid, and — in pregnancy — gain against the appropriate range rather than a target weight.

What we do not do

Work around your obstetrician or fertility specialist. We work alongside them, and we say so in writing.

What we work on

Recovery first. Adequate energy for lactation, iron and B12 repletion after delivery, realistic eating on no sleep, and strength returning before anything else.

What we track

Energy, milk supply where relevant, haemoglobin and ferritin, mood, digestion and a return to strength.

What we do not do

Put weight loss in front of recovery, or add pressure to a body that is already doing a great deal.

What we work on

Protein adequacy, muscle preservation, bone health, sleep, and the metabolic adaptation that makes what used to work stop working.

What we track

Body composition rather than weight, lipids, glucose, vitamin D, calcium intake, sleep quality and symptom pattern.

What we do not do

Tell you it is just age, or hand you the same plan that was written for a twenty-five-year-old.

Who is it for

Women trying to conceive

Pregnant women

Changing metabolism, muscle loss, bone health, sleep disruption and mood.

Women with iron deficiency or anaemia

Extremely prevalent in India, and routinely under-treated.

Problems we solve

What women are usually told, and why it fails

  • PCOS treated as a weight problem rather than a metabolic and endocrine one.
  • 'Eat less, move more' prescribed to a body that is insulin resistant.
  • Thyroid medication issued with no nutrition support alongside it.
  • Cycle, energy and symptoms ignored in favour of the scale.
  • Fertility nutrition starting only after conception has already failed.
  • Postpartum mothers pressured to lose weight while recovering and feeding an infant.
  • Perimenopause dismissed and unmanaged.
  • Iron and vitamin D deficiency so normalised in Indian women that it goes untreated.
  • Restrictive dieting worsening the very hormonal disruption it is meant to fix.

If you recognise four of these, this is a hormonal picture, not a willpower problem.

What we test and track

The panel we read before writing anything

Bring what you already have. Where something is missing and worth doing, Megha will tell you which panel to ask your doctor for and why.

  • Thyroid panel — TSH, free T3, free T4, and antibodies where relevant
  • Fasting insulin and HbA1c, read together rather than separately
  • Fasting glucose and, where indicated, post-meal glucose
  • Lipid profile
  • Vitamin D, vitamin B12, ferritin and haemoglobin
  • Where relevant: testosterone, LH, FSH, prolactin, AMH

Nutrinest interprets reports and adjusts nutrition. Diagnosis and prescription stay with your doctor.

Cycle-aware coaching

Follicular phase. Energy and tolerance for harder training are usually higher. Composition work and new habits land more easily here.

Luteal phase. Appetite, cravings, sleep and body temperature shift. Plans that ignore this get abandoned in the second half of every month, then blamed on willpower.

We plan across both, so the plan survives the whole cycle.

How it works

The Nutrinest approach

Ten stages, in order. Nothing here is optional and nothing here is decoration.

01

Assessment

Cycle history, symptoms, full bloodwork (thyroid panel, insulin, HbA1c, vitamin D, ferritin, B12), medication, medical history and lifestyle.

02

Consultation

First call maps the actual hormonal picture, not just the weight goal.

03

Nutrition Planning

Built for insulin sensitivity, thyroid function, inflammation and nutrient repletion — with adequate energy, never restriction.

04

Lifestyle Coaching

Sleep, stress and strength training are treated as hormonal interventions, because they are.

05

Behaviour Change

Undoing years of restrictive dieting and food fear — often the most important work in this program.

06

Habit Building

Consistent protein, cycle-aware movement, and eating patterns that stabilise blood glucose.

07

Follow-up

Weekly consultations with cycle-phase-aware adjustment.

08

Monitoring

Cycle regularity, symptoms, energy, body composition and repeat bloodwork at milestones.

09

Education

Every client learns her own hormonal picture and how food moves it — the most durable outcome of the program.

10

Long-term Support

Stage transitions supported: PCOS to conception, pregnancy to postpartum, postpartum to perimenopause.

What we do and do not do

Nutrinest does not diagnose medical conditions and does not alter prescribed medication. We work alongside your treating doctor, never in place of them. If something in your history or bloodwork needs medical attention, we will tell you and refer you.
