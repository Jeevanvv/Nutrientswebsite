What We Deliver

The Six Pillars of Our
Corporate Wellness Programme

Every organization is different. These pillars combine into a program shaped around your people, your workplace, and your goals.

Wellness Assessment & Health Screening

Understand your team's health profile before designing any intervention.

  • Body composition & BMI analysis
  • Visceral fat & metabolic age
  • Lifestyle & nutrition assessment
  • Personalized health reports

Nutrition Awareness Programs

Interactive employee sessions that make healthy eating practical.

  • Weight, diabetes & gut health
  • PCOS & hormonal health
  • Office nutrition & smart snacking
  • Stress, sleep & recovery

One-on-One Clinical Consultations

Private, confidential consultations for employees who need individual care.

  • Medical history & diet planning
  • Thyroid, PCOS, hypertension, fatty liver
  • Lifestyle counselling & goal setting
  • Follow-up recommendations

Cafeteria Wellness & Menu Development

Transform the food environment your employees eat in every day.

  • Cafeteria audit & menu review
  • Healthy menu & recipe development
  • Diabetic, heart-healthy & regional options
  • Vendor consultation & implementation

Wellness Challenges & Health Campaigns

Engagement that keeps wellness alive between sessions.

  • 10, 21 & 30-day challenges
  • Hydration, walking & no-sugar challenges
  • Nutrition month & awareness weeks
  • WhatsApp communities & habit tracking

Leadership Wellness & Long-Term Programs

Sustained programs for teams — and exclusive tracks for senior leadership.

  • Executive health & performance nutrition
  • Stress, travel & energy management
  • Monthly reports & progress reviews
  • Quarterly to annual wellness plans
How We Work

Every corporate engagement follows one structured path — so results are measured, not assumed.

1

Assess

Discovery and needs assessment with your HR and leadership team.

2

Analyze

Baseline health evaluation across employees and food environments.

3

Educate

Awareness sessions that build understanding before behaviour change.

4

Personalize

A customized wellness strategy — no copy-paste programs.

5

Implement

6

Track

7

Improve

8

Sustain

Engagement Journey

How the Programme Is Structured

A typical engagement moves through four phases — flexible in duration, consistent in method.

Phase 1

Discovery & Baseline

Needs assessment, health screening camps, body composition analysis, and lifestyle questionnaires establish your organization's starting point.

Phase 2

Strategy & Design

A customized wellness calendar — sessions, consultations, cafeteria interventions, and campaigns mapped to your workforce.

Phase 3

Engagement & Delivery

Awareness programs, one-on-one consultations, wellness challenges, and digital support keep participation high.

Phase 4

Measurement & Partnership

Monthly health reports, management dashboards, and impact analysis — evolving into an annual wellness partnership.

Delivery Models

Onsite Online Hybrid Monthly Retainer Project Based Consultation Based Annual Wellness Partner

Reporting your leadership will actually use

Every engagement delivers health reports, corporate dashboards, cafeteria audit reports, wellness calendars, employee progress reports, and impact analysis — so ROI on wellness is visible, not vague.
