What this is

The flagship personalised transformation journey, led directly by Megha

One-on-One Nutrition Coaching is Nutrinest's flagship offering. It pairs a single client with a clinical nutritionist for a fixed-duration transformation journey built on weekly consultations, personalised nutrition, and behaviour change — not a static diet chart.

Most clients arriving at Nutrinest have already failed two or more diets. They do not need more information; they need personalisation, accountability and someone who adjusts the plan when life changes. Weekly contact is the mechanism that separates a coaching relationship from a downloadable PDF.

How it runs

Delivered online over 3 or 6 months. Each week the client attends a video consultation, submits a food and symptom log, and receives a revised plan. WhatsApp support runs continuously between calls. Bloodwork is reviewed at intake and again at milestone reviews.

What this program sets out to do
  • Deliver a nutrition plan built from the client's own bloodwork, hormones, medical history and lifestyle.
  • Replace short-term dieting with habits the client can hold for life.
  • Course-correct weekly so progress never plateaus.
  • Educate the client so they eventually need us less, not more.
  • Produce measurable change across weight, energy, digestion and biomarkers.

Who is it for

Built for people who have already tried the other thing

Six or seven starting points. Most people arrive at one of them.

Working professionals and entrepreneurs

Long hours, irregular meals, frequent travel and eating out. They need a plan that survives a real calendar, not an ideal one.

Women with PCOS or thyroid disorders

Require hormone-aware nutrition, insulin-sensitivity work and cycle-linked adjustments rather than generic calorie cutting.

People with a long history of failed diets

Have lost and regained weight repeatedly. Need habit reconstruction and a rebuilt relationship with food.

People managing gut health issues

Bloating, irregularity, reflux and food intolerances requiring elimination, reintroduction and structured tracking.

Couples planning pregnancy

Preconception nutrition for both partners, focused on fertility markers and nutrient repletion.

Postpartum mothers

Recovery, lactation support and a realistic return to strength without restriction.

People struggling with emotional eating

Need behavioural coaching alongside nutrition — triggers, patterns and non-food coping.

Problems we solve

What went wrong last time

  • Diet plans that ignore bloodwork, hormones and medical history.
  • Copy-paste meal plans that were never designed for the client's body or kitchen.
  • Rapid loss followed by rapid regain, because nothing behavioural ever changed.
  • Plans that collapse the first time the client travels, attends a wedding or works late.
  • No accountability between the first consultation and the next month.
  • Advice built on Western foods that ignores Indian meal structure and family cooking.
  • Fear-based, shame-based coaching that damages the client's relationship with food.
  • Plateaus that are never diagnosed because nobody is reviewing the data weekly.

If you recognise three of these, this program was built for you.

The weekly difference

A plan written in January cannot know what March does to you

This is the mechanism the whole program rests on.

Bodies shift

Weight, water, digestion and sleep all move week to week. A plan reviewed monthly is working from data that is already four weeks stale.

Hormones shift

Cycle phase, thyroid medication changes and stress all alter what the same meal does. We adjust against where you actually are.

Life shifts

Travel, festivals, deadlines, illness. The plan absorbs them because it is rewritten after the week that actually happened.

Plateaus get diagnosed

A stall is information. Reviewed weekly, it gets identified and corrected in days rather than discovered a month later.

How it works

The Nutrinest approach

Ten stages, in order. Nothing here is optional and nothing here is decoration.

01

Assessment

Detailed intake: medical history, medications, symptoms, sleep, stress, activity, food preferences, kitchen reality and family eating patterns.

02

Consultation

First video call with Megha to establish goals, constraints and a realistic timeline. Objections and past failures are surfaced here, not later.

03

Nutrition Planning

A plan is built from bloodwork, hormone assessment and lifestyle data — portioned, timed and matched to what the client actually cooks and eats.

04

Lifestyle Coaching

Sleep, stress, movement, hydration and meal timing are addressed as first-class levers, not afterthoughts.

05

Behaviour Change

Triggers for emotional and reactive eating are identified and replaced with specific, practised alternatives.

06

Habit Building

One or two habits are installed per phase and tracked to consistency before new ones are added.

07

Follow-up

Weekly video consultation. The plan is revised against the week that actually happened.

08

Monitoring

Weight, measurements, symptoms, energy, digestion, adherence and (where relevant) repeat bloodwork.

09

Education

Every change is explained. The client learns why, so decisions transfer to situations we never planned for.

10

Long-term Support

Exit phase focuses on maintenance, self-management and a clear plan for the six months after the program ends.
