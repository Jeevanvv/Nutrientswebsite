The weekly difference

A plan written in January cannot know what March does to you

Weekly contact is the mechanism that separates a coaching relationship from a downloadable PDF.

Bodies shift

Weight, water, digestion and sleep move week to week. Monthly reviews work from stale data.

Hormones shift

Cycle phase, medication and stress change what the same meal does to you.

Life shifts

Travel, festivals, deadlines and illness are absorbed, not treated as failures.

Plateaus get diagnosed

A stall is information. Reviewed weekly, it is corrected in days.

Meet Megha

You are speaking to a clinical nutritionist, every single week

Megha Kiran is a senior clinical nutritionist, nutrition educator and corporate wellness consultant with an M.Sc. in Dietetics & Applied Nutrition, a child nutrition specialisation, and four years of practice across coaching, community health and corporate programs.

She is also a yoga instructor and a national-level swimmer, which is why the plans she writes assume a body that has to train, travel, work late and eat with its family — not one that lives in a clinic.
