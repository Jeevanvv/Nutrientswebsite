What this is

For the part nobody talks about: everything after the program ends

The Membership Program provides ongoing, low-intensity support for people who have completed a Nutrinest program — sustaining habits, answering questions as they arise, and keeping the community and accountability that transformation was built on.

Every coaching program ends, and the months after are where the results are actually decided. The industry ignores this window entirely, then markets a new program when the person regains. Membership addresses it directly — and commercially, it converts one-off clients into a durable revenue base, which is what makes Nutrinest a business rather than a practice.

How it runs

Monthly or annual subscription. Includes a monthly group call, a private community, monthly content, recipes, seasonal guidance, member-only resources and discounted access to consultations and courses.

What this program sets out to do
  • Prevent the regression that follows the end of every coaching relationship.
  • Provide continuous access at a fraction of the coaching commitment.
  • Build a community that sustains itself between professional touchpoints.
  • Create recurring, predictable revenue independent of consultation hours.
  • Keep graduates connected rather than lost.

Problems we solve

What happens after a program ends

  • The program ends and, within months, so do the habits.
  • Graduates lose the community that made the change possible.
  • Questions arise long after the last consultation, with nowhere to take them.
  • Maintenance is treated as an afterthought by an industry that profits from regain.
  • Restarting a full program is expensive and often unnecessary.
  • Chronic conditions need permanent support and are sold time-boxed programs.
  • Motivation decays without any external structure.
  • The gap between 'coached' and 'entirely alone' is too wide.

If three of these sound like the months after your last plan finished, this is what membership is for.
