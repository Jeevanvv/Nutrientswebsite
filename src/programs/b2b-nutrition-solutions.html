What this is

Nutrition science applied to food products, catalogues and data systems

B2B Nutrition Solutions provides nutrition intelligence to food, retail, quick commerce and digital health companies — classifying products, validating health claims, building nutrition databases and designing the diet filters and health attributes that power food discovery.

Every food platform now makes nutrition claims by implication. A 'healthy' collection, a 'high protein' filter, a 'diabetic-friendly' tag — each is a nutritional assertion made at catalogue scale, usually by someone with no nutrition training. This service puts a qualified nutritionist behind the taxonomy, before a regulator or a journalist asks who decided.

How it runs

Delivered as project-based or retained consulting. Engagements typically begin with an SKU audit or a health attribute framework, and expand into ongoing catalogue and data support.

What this program sets out to do
  • Convert unstructured product data into a defensible nutrition classification system.
  • Validate health claims before they become a regulatory or reputational liability.
  • Build the health attribute frameworks that drive filters, collections and recommendations.
  • Improve food database quality at scale.
  • Give product and category teams a nutrition authority they can point to.

Sample framework

An anonymised health attribute framework

This is the artefact an engagement produces: one attribute set, defined once, applied consistently across every category so that a filter means the same thing everywhere.

AttributeTypeRule appliedApplies to
high_proteinBoolean≥ 10 g protein per 100 g for solids; ≥ 5 g per 100 ml for beverages; protein must be a declared, verifiable valueAll food and beverage SKUs
added_sugar_freeBooleanNo added sugar in the ingredient list, including syrups, juice concentrates and maltodextrinAll SKUs
low_sodiumBoolean≤ 120 mg sodium per 100 g, per the applicable standardAll SKUs
whole_grain_primaryBooleanA whole grain is the first declared ingredient by weightGrains, bakery, snacks
diabetic_suitableDerivedComposite of added sugar, fibre, glycaemic profile and portion; never a single-nutrient callFood SKUs
claim_statusEnumsupportable · unsupported · needs_reviewAny SKU carrying a health claim
data_confidenceEnumverified · declared · estimated — so downstream teams know what they are filtering onAll SKUs

Illustrative thresholds. Real frameworks are set against the applicable regulation, your category mix and your platform's use case.
