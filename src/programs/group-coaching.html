What this is

The Nutrinest method, with a group behind you

Group & Cohort Coaching delivers structured, time-bound programs to small groups moving through the same curriculum together — combining professional coaching with the accountability that only peers produce.

One-on-one coaching is the right answer for complex cases, but it prices out a large audience and is capped by Megha's available hours. Cohorts solve both problems — and for many people, the group is not a compromise. Watching six other women with PCOS hit the same week-three wall is more motivating than any individual pep talk.

How it runs

Delivered online in small cohorts (8--12 people) over 21 days, 30 days, 6 weeks or 12 weeks. Includes live group calls, a shared curriculum, a private group chat and individual check-ins at key milestones.

What this program sets out to do
  • Make Nutrinest coaching accessible at a lower price point without diluting the method.
  • Use peer accountability, which outperforms individual willpower consistently.
  • Deliver condition-specific cohorts where shared context accelerates everyone.
  • Create a scalable delivery model that doesn't depend on one-to-one hours.
  • Provide a natural entry point into one-on-one coaching for those who need more.

Problems we solve

What people are trying to escape

  • Coaching is priced beyond a large, motivated audience.
  • Solo dieting has no witnesses, so quitting costs nothing.
  • Free challenges have no structure, no expertise and no follow-through.
  • People feel isolated in a condition that millions of others share.
  • Motivation collapses in week three and there's nobody there to notice.
  • The gap between a free session and a ₹18,000 program is too wide to step across.
  • Generic group programs are broadcast, not coached.

If three of these are familiar, the missing ingredient was probably accountability, not information.

How a cohort works

Twelve weeks, in a deliberate order

Each week builds on the one before it. Recordings go out the same day for anyone who cannot attend live.

Pre-assessment, bloodwork review, and the core framework: what your plate is doing, why the current pattern produces the current result, and the first habit installed and tracked.

Protein adequacy, fibre, portioning and meal sequencing applied to what you already cook. Group chat becomes the place where real plates get posted and adjusted.

The material that differs by cohort — insulin sensitivity for PCOS, elimination structure for gut health, medication timing and absorption for thyroid.

Travel, eating out, festivals, late nights and the week everything falls apart. Individual milestone check-ins happen here, at the point where most people drop off.

Emotional and reactive eating, trigger mapping, and diagnosing a stall from your own logged data instead of guessing.

Post-assessment, measured change reported to each participant, and a written plan for the six months after the cohort ends.

How it works

The Nutrinest approach

Ten stages, in order. Nothing here is optional and nothing here is decoration.

01

Assessment

Intake questionnaire for every participant, so cohorts are matched by condition and goal rather than assembled at random.

02

Consultation

Group kickoff call plus a short individual onboarding for each participant.

03

Nutrition Planning

A shared framework with individual adjustment. Not a single plan issued to twelve different bodies.

04

Lifestyle Coaching

Weekly group themes on sleep, stress, movement and eating patterns.

05

Behaviour Change

Peer accountability is the mechanism. The group sees your check-in — that changes behaviour more than any plan does.

06

Habit Building

One shared habit per week, tracked publicly within the group.

07

Follow-up

Weekly live group calls with open Q&A, plus milestone individual check-ins.

08

Monitoring

Weekly check-ins, group tracking and pre/post measures.

09

Education

The curriculum is the backbone — every cohort week teaches something specific and cumulative.

10

Long-term Support

Graduates can continue into the Membership Program or step up to one-on-one coaching.
