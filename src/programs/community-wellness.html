What this is

Nutrition education delivered where people already gather

The Community Wellness Program brings preventive nutrition to residential communities, apartment associations, clubs, senior citizen groups and neighbourhood collectives — the places where health behaviour is actually formed and reinforced.

Individual coaching changes one person. Community programs change the environment that person returns to. When a whole apartment block understands portioning, label reading and walking after dinner, adherence stops being an act of willpower.

How it runs

Delivered onsite as a session series or a one-day wellness camp, combining body composition screening, an interactive talk, and optional individual consultation slots. Follow-up runs through a community WhatsApp group and a monthly wellness calendar.

What this program sets out to do
  • Raise baseline nutrition literacy across an entire community, not one household at a time.
  • Screen for undetected metabolic risk in people who never visit a nutritionist.
  • Make healthy behaviour socially normal within the group.
  • Create a repeatable, low-cost entry point into the Nutrinest ecosystem.
  • Support ageing residents with practical, condition-aware guidance.

What happens on the day

A camp runs itself once we are in the room

The committee has to book a hall and tell residents when to arrive. We bring everything else.

Before, 60 minSetup

Screening station, consultation corner and session area set up in your community hall. We need tables, chairs, a plug point and a quiet corner.

Slot 1Body composition screening

Residents cycle through in small groups. Ten measurements per person, roughly four minutes each.

Slot 2Interactive session

45–60 minutes on a topic your committee picks, in plain language, with time for questions that residents actually want answered.

Slot 3Individual consultations

Optional private slots for residents whose readings need a conversation rather than a leaflet.

After, 5–7 daysReports and follow-up

Every participant gets an individual report. The committee gets an aggregate, anonymised picture of the community. The WhatsApp group starts.

Problems we solve

Why one-off health camps change nothing

  • Health advice reaches communities as WhatsApp forwards and marketing claims, not as nutrition science.
  • Metabolic risk goes undetected for years because nobody screens asymptomatic people.
  • Seniors receive restriction lists with no practical replacement guidance.
  • The household cook is never included in the health conversation.
  • One-off health camps generate reports and no behaviour change.
  • Communities equate wellness with a gym membership and stop there.
  • Cultural food practices are dismissed instead of adapted.

How it works

The Nutrinest approach

Ten stages, in order. Nothing here is optional and nothing here is decoration.

01

Assessment

Pre-event survey of the community's age profile, common conditions and food culture.

02

Consultation

Optional private 15-minute slots on the day of the camp for residents who want individual direction.

03

Nutrition Planning

Group-level guidance: portioning, plate composition, meal timing, and condition-specific swaps.

04

Lifestyle Coaching

Practical movement, sleep and hydration targets appropriate to age mix.

05

Behaviour Change

Group challenges create peer accountability that no individual plan can replicate.

06

Habit Building

One community habit per month — hydration, walking, a vegetable at every meal.

07

Follow-up

Community WhatsApp group with weekly tips and a monthly live Q&A.

08

Monitoring

Repeat body composition screening at 3 and 6 months to demonstrate change.

09

Education

Rotating session topics across the wellness calendar so engagement doesn't decay.

10

Long-term Support

Annual community wellness partnership with a fixed calendar of camps and campaigns.
