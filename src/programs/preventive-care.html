What this is

Find it in a report, not in a hospital

The Preventive Care Program is built for people who feel fine. It uses screening, bloodwork interpretation and risk profiling to identify metabolic, cardiovascular and nutritional risk years before symptoms appear — and then does something about it.

India's lifestyle disease burden is not a treatment failure; it is a prevention failure. Prediabetes is reversible and diagnosed diabetes largely isn't. Most people receive an annual health check, glance at the flagged values, file the report and change nothing. This program exists in that gap — between the report and the response.

How it runs

Delivered online as a structured assessment with a follow-up pathway. Includes body composition analysis, complete bloodwork interpretation, family risk profiling, a written risk report and a habit intervention plan. Designed as a lower-commitment entry point into Nutrinest coaching.

What this program sets out to do
  • Detect metabolic and cardiovascular risk while it is still fully reversible.
  • Translate an annual health check from a filed PDF into an actual plan.
  • Correct the deficiencies that quietly degrade energy, immunity and mood.
  • Build a personal risk profile from family history and current markers.
  • Establish protective habits before a diagnosis forces them.

What we assess

The markers that move before symptoms do

  • Fasting glucose, fasting insulin and HbA1c — read together, because one alone misses early insulin resistance
  • Lipid profile, including triglycerides and the ratios that matter more than total cholesterol
  • Liver function and any imaging finding of fatty liver
  • Thyroid panel
  • Vitamin D, vitamin B12, ferritin and haemoglobin
  • Blood pressure and waist circumference
  • Body composition — body fat %, visceral fat, muscle mass, metabolic age
  • Family history, converted into a quantified personal risk picture

Reversible, if it is caught here

Usually reversible: prediabetes, early fatty liver, borderline lipids, most deficiencies, early insulin resistance, borderline blood pressure.

Manageable but not reversible: established type 2 diabetes, advanced liver disease, established cardiovascular disease. The gap between those two lists is usually a few years — which is the entire argument for this program.

What we do and do not do

Nutrinest does not diagnose medical conditions and does not alter prescribed medication. The self-check on this page is an awareness tool, not a diagnostic test. Where a finding needs a doctor, we say so and refer you rather than working around it.
