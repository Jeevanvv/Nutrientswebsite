Legal & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

At NutriNest with Megha, we respect your privacy and take reasonable steps to protect the information you share with us.

Information We Collect

Depending on your interaction with us, we may collect your name, contact details, age, dietary preferences, lifestyle information, weight and measurements, health history, allergies, medication or supplement information, progress information and payment-related details.

How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to:

  • Provide and personalise nutrition services.
  • Prepare and modify your nutrition plan.
  • Manage consultations and follow-ups.
  • Communicate with you through WhatsApp.
  • Process payments and maintain business records.
  • Improve our services and client experience.
  • Meet applicable legal requirements.

Health Information & WhatsApp

  • Health Information: Health and nutrition information is collected only when relevant to providing our services or when voluntarily shared by you. Please ensure that the information you provide is accurate and updated.
  • WhatsApp: WhatsApp is our primary channel for client communication, including plans, follow-ups, updates and support. Information shared through WhatsApp may also be subject to WhatsApp/Meta's privacy practices.

Sharing Information

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • Information may be shared with trusted service providers such as payment processors, website/technology providers or communication platforms when required to operate our services, or where required by law.

Photos & Testimonials

Client photographs, testimonials, transformation stories or identifiable information will not be used publicly for marketing without appropriate permission or consent.

Data Protection & Your Privacy

  • Data Protection: We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, misuse or disclosure. Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for providing services, maintaining records and meeting legal obligations.
  • Your Privacy: Subject to applicable law, you may contact us regarding access, correction, deletion or withdrawal of consent relating to your personal information.

Questions about your privacy?

Reach out to us and we will answer your privacy questions promptly.
