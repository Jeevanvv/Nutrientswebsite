What this is

The front door to everything else

The Healthy Recipe Library is a free, searchable collection of practical Indian recipes filterable by condition, meal type, time and dietary need — designed to be genuinely useful on its own and to serve as the primary organic acquisition channel for the business.

Recipe search is one of the highest-volume, highest-intent categories on the internet, and a woman searching 'PCOS friendly breakfast Indian' is close to the ideal Nutrinest client. Every other program page needs an audience sent to it. This one builds the audience. Strategically, it is the top of the entire funnel — and it should be resourced accordingly, not treated as a blog.

How it runs

A free, permanently available, continuously growing library on the website. Filterable, searchable, mobile-optimised and schema-marked. Expanded content and collections are available to members.

What this program sets out to do
  • Rank for high-volume recipe search and bring qualified organic traffic to the site.
  • Demonstrate the Nutrinest philosophy through food rather than argument.
  • Convert readers into email subscribers and, over time, clients.
  • Give existing clients and members a practical, growing resource.
  • Establish nutritional authority through utility rather than claims.

Problems we solve

Why most healthy recipe sites are useless

  • 'Healthy' recipes online are usually Western, unaffordable or inauthentic.
  • Recipes labelled diabetic-friendly or PCOS-friendly with no nutritional basis whatsoever.
  • Nutrition information absent, estimated or invented.
  • Recipes requiring ingredients unavailable in an ordinary Indian market.
  • Endless narrative before the actual recipe, because the site is optimised for ads rather than cooks.
  • No filtering by condition, so people scroll through irrelevant results.
  • Portions unstated, making the nutrition data meaningless.
  • Recipes that photograph well and cook badly.

If three of these have annoyed you elsewhere, this library was built in response to them.

About the nutrition numbers

Values are per the stated portion and are calculated from the ingredients rather than estimated from a similar dish. Portions are the ones people actually serve, not a 30 g reference nobody uses.

A recipe tagged for a condition is suitable in the context of an overall plan. It is not a treatment, and one good breakfast does not replace one.
