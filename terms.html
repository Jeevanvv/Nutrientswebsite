Client Guidelines

Program Terms & Client Protocol

Welcome to NutriNest!

Thank you for choosing to work with us. Please read the following guidelines carefully. These terms help us maintain a smooth, structured and effective program experience for every client.

1. PROGRAM ACTIVATION

  • Your program must be started within 45 days from the date of payment.
  • Once the program has been activated, the program period will run according to the agreed package duration.

2. CONSULTATIONS & RESCHEDULING

  • Consultation calls can be rescheduled a maximum of 3–4 times during the program.
  • Rescheduling must be communicated in advance and is subject to appointment availability.
  • Missed appointments/no-shows or last-minute cancellations may be considered as a completed consultation.
  • Please be punctual for scheduled consultations so that your session can be completed effectively.

3. PROGRAM TRANSFER

  • The program is personalised specifically for the enrolled client and therefore cannot be transferred to another person after 30 days from payment.
  • Any transfer request, if applicable, must be discussed with NutriNest and approved before the program is transferred.

4. PERSONALISED PLANS & SUPPORT

  • Diet plans and recommendations are personalised based on the information, goals, preferences and health details shared by the client.
  • Plans may be modified during the program based on progress, feedback, travel, lifestyle changes and other relevant factors.

5. CLIENT RESPONSIBILITY

To make the program effective, clients are expected to:

  • Share accurate health and lifestyle information.
  • Follow the recommended plan consistently.
  • Provide requested weight/progress updates on time.
  • Inform the nutritionist about travel, illness, medication changes or other relevant circumstances.
  • Communicate concerns or difficulties instead of discontinuing the plan without discussion.

6. PROGRAM PAUSE / EXTENSION

  • The program does not automatically get extended due to personal travel, work commitments, holidays or periods of inactivity.
  • Any exceptional pause or extension must be discussed and approved by NutriNest in advance.

7. PAYMENT & REFUND

  • All payments are non-refundable once completed, as payment confirms enrolment and secures your dedicated program slot.
  • Upon payment, NutriNest allocates professional time, consultation slots, administrative resources and personalised program preparation specifically for you.
  • Please clarify any questions regarding the program, inclusions, duration and terms before making payment, as cancellations or changes after payment will not qualify for a refund.

8. HEALTH & RESULTS

  • Nutrition guidance provided by NutriNest is intended to support your health and wellness and does not replace medical diagnosis or treatment.
  • Please disclose all relevant medical conditions, medications, allergies, pregnancy/breastfeeding status and other important health information before starting.
  • Individual results vary depending on multiple factors including consistency, lifestyle, health status and adherence. Specific results cannot be guaranteed.

9. COMMUNICATION

  • All program-related communication, updates, follow-ups and support will be provided exclusively through WhatsApp.
  • Clients are expected to check WhatsApp regularly and respond to messages or requests from the NutriNest team.
