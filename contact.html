Practical details

  • Where: Bengaluru, India — consultations delivered online across India and 10+ countries
  • Response time: one working day, usually sooner
  • Consultation length: 45 minutes for a first call
  • What to bring: blood work from the last six months, current medication, and a rough picture of a normal week of eating
  • Programs: ₹18,000 for three months, ₹24,000 for six months, both with weekly video consultations

Before you book

Nutrinest does not diagnose medical conditions and does not alter prescribed medication. We work alongside your treating doctor. If you are currently unwell, pregnant with complications, or managing a condition under active treatment, tell us on the call — it changes what we recommend, and occasionally it means we recommend nothing at all.

Tell us what you have already tried
