Legal

Privacy policy

What we collect, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Draft — not yet reviewed

This page is a working draft written to sit correctly in the site structure. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer and must not be published as-is. Have it checked against India’s DPDP Act 2023 and your own data practices before launch.

What we collect

Contact details you give us through an enquiry or booking form: name, email, phone number, city and what you have asked for help with.

Health information you share as a client: medical history, medications, symptoms, blood reports, measurements, food and lifestyle logs.

Basic usage data from this website, such as pages viewed. We do not use advertising trackers.

Why we collect it

To respond to your enquiry, deliver the program you have signed up for, and keep an accurate clinical record of the guidance we have given you.

Health data is used to build and revise your nutrition plan. It is never sold, and it is never shared with an employer, an insurer or a marketing partner.

Corporate and institutional engagements

Individual employee or student health reports go to that person only. Anything shared with the organisation is aggregate and anonymised, and this is fixed contractually before an engagement begins.

How long we keep it

Client records are retained for the period required for continuity of care and applicable record-keeping obligations, then deleted. Enquiry details that do not become an engagement are deleted after a defined period.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Write to info@nutrinestwithmegha.com and we will confirm within a reasonable period.
